# 13 · Glass pavilion

A leather chair in a glass room above a lake, black mirror slabs, eroded snowy mountains outside.
One slow 20 s camera move from the front of the chair to behind it.

Everything in the frame is generated by `scene.py`, about 440 lines of Python for Blender:
no downloaded models, no textures, no photos, no HDRI.

- **Chair**: side profiles extruded into one upholstered shell, three tufted back channels, bevel + subdivision, steel legs. Leather is noise-driven colour and roughness with a grain bump.
- **Terrazzo**: four layers of Voronoi stone chips over a warm noise base.
- **Mountains**: a 2,600 m heightfield, 1400×1400 vertices (about 2 million). Ridged noise where each octave is damped by the slope built so far, so valleys stay smooth and ridges get sharp. Snow follows slope, altitude and fall-line streaks; rock gets strata and crevices.
- **Light**: a physical sky plus a low evening sun through the glass. Distant terrain fades into the same sky along each view ray.
- **Render**: Cycles path tracing, motion blur, depth of field; `post.py` adds bloom, a warm grade, vignette and grain.
- **Sound**: `audio.py` synthesizes wind on glass, a low pad and a few chimes.

## Run it

```
blender -b --python scene.py -- out 0,240,470 1280 32        # three preview frames on CPU
HQ=1 BUBO_GPU=1 blender -b --python scene.py -- full 0:480:1 1920 128
python3 post.py full graded && python3 audio.py
```

`colab_render.sh` does the whole thing on a Colab GPU (L4): installs Blender 5.0.1, renders 480 frames to Google Drive
(a rerun resumes after a disconnect), grades them and writes a 1920×804 mp4 with sound. About an hour on an L4.
